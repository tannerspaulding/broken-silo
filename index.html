Broken Silo

Data you can hear and see. Each track is one public dataset you play with your finger.

  1. i

    Arrivals

    Every day of the year, by how many babies are born on it.

  2. ii

    Twelve Hundred Springs

    Kyoto's cherry blossoms, every recorded spring since 812.

  3. iii

    Weathering

    Ten homes, one year of rain from each, as beat pads.

  4. iv

    Stoops

    Baltimore's lived-in homes, block by block, oldest to newest.

  5. v

    Light Rail

    A ride down the line, each stop as busy as its weekday riders.

  6. vi

    Murals

    Baltimore's public art, oldest to newest, heard from where you stand.
