# Broken Silo

Data you can hear and see. A test press of six playable tracks, built by [Tanner Spaulding](https://tannerspaulding.com). Each track turns a public dataset into music and a moving picture you can play with.

- **Arrivals:** U.S. births per day, 2000 to 2014. Social Security Administration, via FiveThirtyEight (CC BY 4.0).
- **Twelve Hundred Springs:** Kyoto cherry full-flowering dates, 812 to 2026, compiled by Yasuyuki Aono (copyright Yasuyuki Aono), via the George Mason University peak bloom prediction project. Used for a free, non-commercial site.
- **Weathering:** daily rain and high temperature from NOAA's Global Historical Climatology Network-Daily (Menne et al. 2012, doi:10.7289/V5D21VHZ), nearest station to each place.
- **Stoops:** every Baltimore neighborhood with 60 or more lived-in homes, oldest to newest by the average year its homes were built. Real Property Information, Baltimore City (Open Baltimore, CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), from the November 2025 copy in the baltimore-real-property project on GitHub, which keeps lived-in residential homes with a recorded sale. Beaver Dam quarry date from Baltimore Magazine, "Anatomy of a Baltimore Rowhome" (2025). The sounds are made in code.
- **Light Rail:** a ride down Baltimore's Light Rail, each of its 33 stops as busy as its average weekday riders. Station ridership from the Maryland Transit Administration via Maryland iMap, through the mapbaltimore project by Eli Pousson (MIT).
- **Murals:** 1,091 dated works of Baltimore public art, oldest to newest, louder the nearer they are to where you listen. The public art inventory by Eli Pousson and C. Ryan Patterson with CHAP, Baltimore Heritage and BOPA (updated January 2023), through the mapbaltimore project (MIT).

Light Rail and Murals can use your location if you choose to share it. Inside Baltimore you start near where you are, and elsewhere your direction from the city sets where you enter. Your location stays in your browser and is never sent or kept.

How each track is made is in [guide.html](guide.html), the "How it's made" page on the site.
